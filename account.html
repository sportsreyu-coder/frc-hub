Choosing as: Mentor

Create a team

The first mentor on a team is its admin; co-mentors who join later need to be granted admin rights by an existing admin. Co-mentors and students can join later with the separate join codes you'll get after creating.

Join a team

Ask your mentor for the join code or link — students and mentors use different codes, so the right role is set automatically.
