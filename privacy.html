Privacy Policy

What FRC Hub collects, why, who can see it, and how to download or delete it. Last updated November 4, 2026.

1. Who we are

FRC Hub is a small, community-run toolkit for FIRST® Robotics Competition teams, maintained by volunteers. It is not an official FIRST product and is not affiliated with FIRST. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the FRC Hub maintainers. See also our Terms.

2. If you never sign in

You can browse FRC Hub without an account. Your Season Tracker progress, saved grants, and theme choice are then stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and aren’t sent to our database. Clearing your browser data removes them.

3. Information we collect when you sign in

Account details. You can sign in with Google, with an email and password, or with an emailed link. We store your email address to identify your account. With Google, we also receive your name and profile picture. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, and we never receive your Google password.

Profile. Your display name, avatar color, profile picture, forum signature, team number and name, district, and reminder-email preference.

Team content. If you join a team: your role, subteam, and captain roles, plus what your team adds to the shared tools, such as Season Tracker progress, tasks and assignments, build-log time entries and mentor notes, budget entries, sponsors, saved grants, deadlines, and notes.

Invitations. If a teammate invites you by email, we store your address with the invite and send the invitation to it.

Forum activity. Your posts, replies, and any reports you submit.

4. Information collected automatically

Our hosting and database providers may log technical data such as IP addresses, browser type, and request times to keep the service secure and reliable. FRC Hub has no advertising or analytics trackers. We use browser storage only to keep you signed in and remember your preferences.

5. How we use information

We use your information to run your account and team tools, sync your data across devices, show your identity on forum posts and to teammates, email you invitations and reminders 14, 7, and 1 days before deadlines for items assigned to you (you can turn reminders off), moderate the forum, keep the service secure, fix problems, and respond to requests. We don’t use it for advertising.

6. What is public

Forum posts and replies can be read by anyone, including people who aren’t signed in, and appear with your team number, team name, avatar color, and signature.

Profile information is also publicly readable. That includes your display name, team number and name, district, avatar color, profile picture, signature, and reminder preference. Our pages mostly show your team instead of your name, but someone could retrieve these fields directly. Your email address and password aren’t part of your profile.

If you signed in with Google or another method that supplied your name, your display name may start out as that name. Students especially should change it to a nickname or first name on the Account page.

7. Who else can see your information

Teammates can see your display name, avatar, role, subteam, captain roles, and your team’s shared data, including the work assigned to you. Mentors can review student assignments and manage the team.

Calendar feeds. If your team subscribes to its calendar feed, anyone who has that private link can see your team’s task and grant deadline titles, so treat it like a password.

Maintainers have administrative access to the database and handle forum reports, and they may view account data when needed for moderation, support, or security.

8. Students and minors

Many FRC Hub users are students, including some under 18. We don’t ask for your age, home address, or phone number.

FRC Hub is intended for FRC team members and isn’t directed to children under 13. There is no separate parental-consent process, so if you’re under 13, please don’t create an account or post without a parent or guardian’s permission and help. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without that consent, contact us and we’ll delete it. Parents and guardians can also ask us to review or delete their child’s data.

9. Service providers and sharing

We don’t sell your personal information or share it with advertisers. We use these providers to run the site, and they handle data as needed to provide their services: Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage), Google (optional sign-in), Resend (invitation and reminder emails), and Vercel (hosting). Google Fonts and jsDelivr supply fonts and scripts, so they receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or if needed to protect the safety of users or the service.

10. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Self-serve deletion is described in the next section. Content that belongs to your team stays with the team after you leave it, and team data can remain until the team is removed. Provider logs and backups may persist for a limited time after deletion.

11. Your choices and rights

Edit. Change your display name, avatar, signature, and reminder preference on the Account page.

Download. Under Privacy & data on the Account page, “Download my data” gives you a file with your account details, profile, personal season data, and team summary. It doesn’t include forum posts or shared team content, which you can request from us.

Delete. After leaving any team, “Delete my account data” removes your profile, forum posts and replies, personal season data, and uploaded profile picture. It does not remove your underlying sign-in record (such as your email address) or team data. To have those removed, use the “Contact” link in the footer and tell us which email you signed in with.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA may give you further rights, such as objecting to certain processing or complaining to a data protection authority.

12. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access rules that limit private data to the people permitted to see it. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security. Please keep your sign-in details safe and tell us about any suspected problem.

13. International users

FRC Hub and its providers may process data in the United States and other countries, whose data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

15. Contact

Email us at sportsreyu@gmail.com, or use the “Contact” link in the footer of any page.
