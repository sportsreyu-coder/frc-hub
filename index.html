Questions teams ask first

Is it really free?

Yes. FRC Hub is a community project with no paid tier. You can browse grants and scholarships without an account; signing in adds saving, sync, and team features.

Is this run by FIRST?

No. FRC Hub is community-maintained and not affiliated with FIRST®. Always confirm details on the funder's own site before you apply.

How current is the grant data?

Each grant shows when it was last verified. If something's wrong, the "Report incorrect info" link in the footer goes straight to the maintainers.

Do students need their own accounts?

Only if they want assignments and the shared team view. A mentor can create the team and invite students by email.
