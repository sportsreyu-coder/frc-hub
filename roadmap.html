Roadmap

What's built today, and what's coming next.

Built so far

A grant finder with eligibility filtering and saved grants, a season tracker with a shared team checklist and calendar, fundraising and scholarship directories, sponsorship resources, a district forum, and team accounts with mentor/student roles.

Next up

Sponsor and budget tracking, a grant application pipeline (move a saved grant through Interested → Drafting → Submitted → Awarded), and deadline reminders. This dashboard is where they'll show up once they land.
