What week is your first competition?

Week 1 is the standard ~7-week build season already built into this schedule. Pick a later week if your first event isn't until then — the extra weeks go mostly toward more prototyping, CAD, and programming time; fabrication, assembly, wiring, and the final driver-practice stretch stay about as tight as they are today.
